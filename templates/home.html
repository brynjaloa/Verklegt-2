ArtVault - est. 2026

ArtVault is a web-based art auction platform designed to connect artists, collectors, and buyers in a seamless digital environment. The system allows users to browse artworks, place bids in ongoing auctions, and manage their personal profiles. Artists can list their work for sale, while buyers can track auctions and participate in real time. The platform focuses on intuitive navigation, clear user flows, and an engaging visual design to ensure a smooth and accessible user experience for both new and experienced users.
