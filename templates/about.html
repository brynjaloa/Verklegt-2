Our story

ArtVault was founded in 2026 by a group of 3 students who wanted to create a seamless digital marketplace for art. We believe that great art should be accessible to everyone, and our platform connects artists directly with buyers around the world.

Our mission is to provide a transparent, secure, and intuitive platform where artists can list their work and buyers can bid with confidence.


Trustworthy team

Our team consists of passionate individuals with backgrounds in art, technology, and business. We are committed to building a platform that serves both artists and collectors with integrity and care.

Every artwork listed on ArtVault goes through a verification process to ensure authenticity and quality. We stand behind every transaction made on our platform.
